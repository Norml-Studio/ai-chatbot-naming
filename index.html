Three directions to react to first.

These are the strongest candidates after removing obvious public product collisions. They are not trademark-cleared names.

  1. 01

    Answerloom

    Answerloom — AI Assistant for WordPress

    Answers woven from the website’s own material. It is the clearest expression of the product’s current promise and still leaves room for a more capable agent later.

    Watch for
    A slight craft association around “loom.”
  2. 02

    Sourcekind

    Sourcekind — AI Assistant for WordPress

    The balance of grounded sources and considerate behavior. It has the best potential to become a broader product company rather than a feature label.

    Watch for
    “Source kind” is already technical terminology.
  3. 03

    Boundwise

    Boundwise — Trusted AI Assistant for WordPress

    A strong trust position for sensitive sites. Its point of view is that a good assistant knows both what it can answer and when to hand someone over.

    Watch for
    It can initially read as compliance software.
