WP Concierge

AI assistant for website visitors.↓Answers from the site and knowledge base. Brings in a person when needed.

A WordPress-first product that helps businesses understand what their visitors need next.

An AI concierge, not a call agent.

WP Concierge gives a visitor a helpful first response while keeping the business in control. It turns approved website content and knowledge into a conversation, then lets the right person join when the conversation needs judgment.

Answers from your website and knowledge base.

Human help when it matters.

Insight into what visitors need next.

Three jobs in one visitor conversation.

The product is useful to the visitor in the moment, useful to the team when attention is needed, and useful to the business after the conversation ends.

  1. 01

    Help the visitor

    Website + knowledge-base answers

    Give a relevant answer, explain an option, and guide the visitor to the right next step using the business’s approved information.

    Outcome: fewer dead ends on the site.

  2. 02

    Bring in the team

    Human handoff + desktop notification

    Notify the right person and let them take over an active conversation without making the visitor start again.

    Outcome: help stays human when it should.

  3. 03

    Improve the site

    Daily visitor insight digest

    Show what visitors asked, where they were unclear, and which content or answers the business should create or improve next.

    Outcome: the site gets more useful over time.
