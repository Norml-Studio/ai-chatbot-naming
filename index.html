Eight directions, not one forced style.

The field deliberately spans practical product titles, compact roles, conversational mechanics, guidance, mythology, and the sharper WP-adjacent energy behind the “Warp” thought.

  1. 01

    Front of house

    Foyer · Usher · Steward · Reception

    The product as the helpful first presence a visitor meets—then a human can take over.

    Feel: professional and human.

  2. 02

    Conversation relay

    Relay · Bridge · Thread · First Reply

    The product as a conversation that can continue from AI to a person without losing context.

    Feel: product-native and behavior-led.

  3. 03

    Guidance

    Beacon · Sage · Compass · Aide

    The product as reliable help for a visitor trying to understand, choose, or act.

    Feel: calm and credible.

  4. 04

    Mythic messenger

    Iris · Ariadne · Maia · Mimir

    Existing cultural roles—messenger, guide, keeper of knowledge—rather than synthetic SaaS words.

    Feel: distinctive, descriptor-dependent.

Twelve useful reactions.

Not a ranked shortlist. These names make the direction choices tangible. A reaction to one is more useful than a premature choice of one.

Relay

Strongest expression of AI-to-human continuation.
